Privacy Policy — The Stoic Porch

Effective 6 September 2026

The Stoic Porch is a meditation app made by one person, Danny. This policy is short because the app collects nothing.

What the app stores

Everything the app knows lives on your phone and nowhere else:

  • which days you sat, and which meditation you sat with
  • the one-line reflections you choose to keep
  • your voice choice and, if you set one, the time of the morning bell

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. The app does not back it up. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party code that watches what you do.
  • One download per meditation. The recordings are too large to ship inside the app, so the first time you open a day's sitting the app fetches it from my storage bucket (Supabase) and keeps it on your phone. Like any download, that request shows Supabase your phone's IP address; it carries nothing else, no account, no identifier, and I keep no log of who fetched what. Nothing is ever uploaded.
  • No access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or location.

Notifications

If you set the morning bell, the app schedules a reminder on your phone. It is local: no push service, no server, and I never know whether you set one. Turn it off with “silence the bell”, or in your phone's settings.

Links that leave the app

Two links open other services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • “leave a coffee” opens Buy Me a Coffee in your browser.
  • “write to Danny” opens your mail app with a draft addressed to me. If you send it, I will see your email address and what you wrote, and I will use them only to reply.

Crash reports from the app stores

While the app is in testing, Apple (TestFlight) and Google (Play Console) may share crash and performance reports with me under their own terms. These contain technical details about the crash, not your reflections.

Children

The Stoic Porch is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting anything — for instance, to sync reflections between your devices — this policy will change first, and the app will tell you before it happens.
